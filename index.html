How to use Pigeonhole

Pigeonhole gives you free throwaway email addresses. Use one whenever a website wants your email and you'd rather not give your real one, for example to receive a sign-up code or a download link. There's no account or password. Everything is saved in this browser.

What each button does

Copy
Copies your current address so you can paste it anywhere.
New address
Makes a fresh random address. Your old ones stay in the row of tabs above your emails, so you can switch back.
Choose name
Pick the first part of the address yourself, such as jane.test, and choose an ending from the list.
Delete inbox
Permanently removes the current address and all its emails. Click it twice to confirm.
Code (yellow)
Appears when an email contains a sign-in or verification code. Click to copy the code.
Link (blue)
Appears when an email has a "verify" or "confirm" link. Click to open it.
Show images
Pictures in emails are hidden at first so senders can't tell you opened their email. Click to show them.
Alerts
Plays a sound and shows a pop-up when new email arrives. Your browser may ask for permission.
Backup
Gives you a code that saves all your addresses. Paste it into Pigeonhole on another browser and click Restore to get them back.

Good to know

  • Receive only. You can't send or reply from Pigeonhole.
  • Same browser, same inboxes. A different browser, a private window, or clearing your browsing data won't show your addresses. Use Backup to keep them.
  • Emails don't last forever. Old messages are cleared after a while.
  • Not for important accounts. Don't use it for banking, your main accounts, or anything you might need to recover later.

Something not working?

  • No address shows up: check your internet connection and refresh the page.
  • "This inbox has expired": the address was removed. Click New address.
  • Email hasn't arrived after a few minutes: some websites block throwaway addresses. Try a new address, or pick a different ending with Choose name.

Backup and restore

Your inboxes live only in this browser. Copy this backup code somewhere safe to restore them later, or paste a code from another device and press Restore. Anyone with the code can read these inboxes.
