Connect your Android phone

Pigeonhole Texts shows the text messages on your Android phone here on your computer, and lets you reply. Your phone does the sending, so texts come from your real number. It uses the free textbee app to link the two.

  1. Put your SIM in an Android phoneAny Android phone works, even an old spare one. iPhones aren't supported.
  2. Install textbee on the phoneGo to textbee.dev, create a free account, and download the app. Open it and allow it to send and receive SMS.
  3. Link the phoneIn the textbee dashboard on your computer, click Register device and scan the QR code with the textbee app. Make sure Receive SMS is switched on in the app.
  4. Get your API keyIn the dashboard, click Generate API key and copy it.
  5. Paste it on the rightYour key is saved only in this browser. Keep the phone charged and connected to Wi-Fi.

Free textbee accounts can send 50 texts a day and 300 a month. Receiving is unlimited.

How to use Pigeonhole Texts

Reading texts

  • Conversations are listed on the left, newest first. A blue dot means a new text you haven't opened.
  • Click a conversation to read it. Your messages are on the right in blue; theirs are on the left.
  • If a text contains a sign-in or verification code, a yellow Code button appears. Click it to copy the code.
  • New texts appear on their own within about 10 seconds. Turn on Alerts for a sound and pop-up.

Sending texts

  • To reply, type in the box at the bottom of a conversation and press Send (or Ctrl + Enter).
  • To text someone new, click New, type their number (for example 07700 900123), then your message.
  • Under each sent text you'll see its progress: Queued, Sent, Delivered, or Failed.
  • A text over 160 characters is split into parts and may cost more on your phone plan. The counter shows how many parts.

Good to know

  • Your phone must be on, charged and online. The bar at the top shows when it last checked in.
  • Texts cost what your phone plan charges. Most UK plans include unlimited texts.
  • Free textbee limit: 50 sent texts a day and 300 a month.
  • Keep this page private. Your key is saved in this browser only. Click Disconnect on shared computers.
  • Only texts received after textbee was set up appear here. Older texts stay on the phone.

Something not working?

  • "Phone offline": open the textbee app on the phone, check it's connected to the internet, and turn off battery saving for textbee.
  • Texts arrive on the phone but not here: in the textbee app, make sure Receive SMS is switched on.
  • "Key not accepted": generate a new API key in the textbee dashboard and connect again.
Texts are sent and received by your own phone through textbee. Your key never leaves this browser except to talk to textbee.
